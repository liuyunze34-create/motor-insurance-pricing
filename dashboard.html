Modeling notes

The actuarial baselines use Poisson claim frequency, Gamma conditional severity and a direct Tweedie pure-premium model. The nonlinear challenger uses histogram gradient boosting for frequency and severity. Exposure weights frequency and pure-premium models; claim count weights severity.

Severity remains the weakest component. The top 1% of positive-loss policies account for about 28.7% of modeled claim amount even after the €200,000 policy loss cap.

Pricing relativities in the repository are prediction-based around a fixed reference policy rather than raw one-hot coefficients.

Portfolio exercise only. Pure premium excludes expenses, profit, tax, capital and regulatory adjustments.
